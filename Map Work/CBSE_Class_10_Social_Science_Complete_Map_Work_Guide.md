## Part 1: History — Nationalism in India (2 Marks)

### I. Indian National Congress Sessions (Locating & Identification)
1. **Calcutta (Sep. 1920)**: West Bengal — *Adoption of Non-Cooperation Movement resolution*
2. **Nagpur (Dec. 1920)**: Maharashtra — *Adoption and final endorsement of the Non-Cooperation programme*
3. **Madras (1927)**: Tamil Nadu — *Resolution to boycott the Simon Commission*

### II. Important Centres of the National Movement
*(Three Satyagraha movements, Jallianwala Bagh, and Dandi March)*
1. **Champaran (Bihar)**: Movement of Indigo Planters (First Satyagraha in India, 1917)
2. **Kheda (Gujarat)**: Peasant Satyagraha against revenue collection during crop failure (1917)
3. **Ahmedabad (Gujarat)**: Cotton Mill Workers Satyagraha for wage hike (1918)
4. **Amritsar (Punjab)**: Jallianwala Bagh Massacre (13 April 1919)
5. **Dandi (Gujarat)**: Salt March end point / Breaking of Salt Law, launch of Civil Disobedience Movement (6 April 1930)

## Part 2: Geography (3 Marks)

### Chapter 1: Resources and Development
* **Major Soil Types (Identification Only)**:
  1. **Alluvial Soil**: Northern Plains (Punjab, Haryana, UP, Bihar, WB) and coastal deltas of Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri.
  2. **Black Soil (Regur)**: Deccan Trap region (Maharashtra, Saurashtra, Malwa, MP, Chhattisgarh).
  3. **Red and Yellow Soil**: Parts of Odisha, Chhattisgarh, southern middle Ganga plain, Western Ghats piedmont zone.
  4. **Laterite Soil**: Western Ghats region of Karnataka, Kerala, MP, hilly areas of Odisha and Assam.
  5. **Arid Soil**: Western Rajasthan.
  6. **Forest / Mountain Soil**: Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh.

## 🎯 Pro-Tips for Scoring 5/5 on the Map Question:
1. **Always use a sharp HB pencil**: Do not use ink or gel pens on the map.
2. **Label in capital letters**: It ensures handwriting clarity and prevents misinterpretation by the evaluator.
3. **Accurate arrow pointer**: Draw a neat, short arrow from your dot to your text if the space on the map is tight.
4. **State Boundary Familiarity**: Memorize the location relative to state boundaries (e.g., Narora is in Western UP near Delhi; Tarapur is on the Northern Maharashtra coastline; Bhakra Nangal is near the HP-Punjab border).
